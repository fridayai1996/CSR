District Overview & Administration

Formed on 4 April 2022 from the Rayalaseema region, centered around Puttaparthi and Penukonda.

Sri Sathya Sai district is located in the southern Rayalaseema region of Andhra Pradesh, India. Named after Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, the district is celebrated for pioneering institutions that brought specialized free healthcare, advanced drinking water networks, and higher education universities to rural Rayalaseema. The administrative headquarters is at Puttaparthi.

Geography, Rivers & Mineral Reserves

The district is bounded by Anantapur district to the north, Annamayya and YSR Kadapa districts to the east, and by Chitradurga, Tumakuru, and Chikkaballapura districts of Karnataka to the west and south-west. Five perennial and seasonal river basins traverse the terrain:

•Pennar River Basin
•Chitravathi River Basin
•Jayamangala River Basin
•Papagni River Basin
•Maddileru River Basin
•Red & Black Soil Belts

Mineral deposits include high-grade quartz, commercial granite, pyrophyllite, and gold reserves in Ramagiri mandal.

Industrial Corridors & Manufacturing Anchors

Strategically positioned along the Bengaluru-Hyderabad NH-44 Industrial Corridor.

Automotive Hub Penukonda

Penukonda Automobile Manufacturing Complex

State-of-the-art 536-acre mega manufacturing facility at Yerramanchi village with 3.5 Lakh annual vehicle production capacity, supporting over 40 tier-1 supplier ancillaries.

Specialty Chemicals Hindupur

Hindupur Industrial & Coatings Park

Large-scale automated paints and industrial coatings manufacturing complex generating direct local employment and industrial skilling.

Textile & Apparel Gollapuram

Gollapuram Apparel & Textile Cluster

Premier textile manufacturing cluster employing over 5,000 rural women weavers and operators across Hindupur and Somandepalli mandals.
