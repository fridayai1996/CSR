Sri Sathya Sai District Development Profile

Formed from Dharmavaram, Penukonda, Kadiri, Madakasira, and Puttaparthi revenue divisions, Sri Sathya Sai District encompasses 32 mandals and 467 villages with headquarters at Puttaparthi. Named after Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, the district holds a renowned heritage of social service, specialized education, and healthcare institutions.

While industrial corridors in Penukonda and textile clusters in Dharmavaram drive economic dynamism, persistent socioeconomic gaps remain across rural habitations: safe drinking water access, diagnostic equipment in Primary Health Centres, modernization of welfare hostels, and digital classroom infrastructure in government schools.
