Sri Sathya Sai District Administration

Formed on 4 April 2022 from Dharmavaram, Penukonda, Kadiri, Madakasira, and Puttaparthi revenue divisions, Sri Sathya Sai District encompasses 32 mandals and 467 villages with headquarters at Puttaparthi. Named after Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, the district is celebrated for its legacy of social service, specialized education, and super-speciality health institutions.

Under the leadership of District Collector Sri A. Shyam Prasad, I.A.S and Joint Collector Sri Mantri Mourya Bharadwaj, I.A.S, our CSR Facilitation Cell works closely with corporate partners to deliver transparent, high-impact projects in safe water supply, smart education, mobile healthcare, and solar energy.
